# Content Guidelines

## Voice

AfrizZenith should communicate like a confident professional company.

The tone should be:

-   Clear
-   Human
-   Direct
-   Intelligent
-   Warm
-   Confident
-   Business-focused

Avoid sounding:

-   Corporate for the sake of corporate language
-   Overly enthusiastic
-   Robotic
-   Salesy
-   Generic
-   AI-generated

## Copy Principles

Prefer:

> We help businesses build, grow and scale through digital
> transformation.

Over:

> We empower businesses to unlock unprecedented digital possibilities
> through cutting-edge innovation.

The first communicates a real service.

The second sounds generic.

## Marketing Language

Avoid repetitive use of:

-   Transform
-   Revolutionize
-   Empower
-   Unlock
-   Seamless
-   Cutting-edge
-   Next-generation
-   Innovative
-   Disruptive
-   Game-changing
-   Unparalleled
-   World-class

These words are not prohibited, but they should not become default
filler.

## Specificity

Prefer concrete statements.

Weak:

> We create powerful digital experiences.

Better:

> We design websites, digital products and systems that help businesses
> operate and grow.

## Claims

Never invent:

-   Revenue numbers
-   Customer counts
-   Success rates
-   Years of experience
-   Awards
-   Certifications
-   Client names
-   Partnerships
-   Testimonials

Only use claims supplied by the business or explicitly approved by the
user.

## Headings

Headings should communicate the purpose of the section.

Good:

-   Our Core Values
-   How We Work
-   The Why Behind AfrizZenith
-   Let's Build Something

Avoid headings that exist only to sound clever.

## CTAs

Use clear actions.

Good:

-   Let's Work Together
-   Start a Project
-   Send a Message
-   Contact Us

Avoid vague CTAs such as:

-   Discover More
-   Explore the Future
-   Unlock Possibilities

unless they genuinely fit the context.

## Content Length

Do not add copy simply to fill visual space.

Short, useful copy is preferable to paragraphs that say little.

## Existing Content

When approved content is provided:

-   Preserve wording.
-   Preserve meaning.
-   Do not rewrite automatically.
-   Do not "improve" the copy without instruction.

## Accessibility

Content must remain understandable without relying on visual styling.

Do not use:

-   Decorative text as the only explanation
-   Icon-only meaning
-   Color-only states
-   Excessive uppercase text
